◆ Our Story

Named for a mountain, built for the journey up to it

"Niladree" — the blue mountain of Puri — is where the company's first tours ran. The name stayed as the business grew into international and student travel too.

◆ Since 2014

What started as one bus, one route

Niladree Radiant began with a single annual departure to Puri, organised for a few hundred pilgrims from local temple committees. Over a decade, that grew into fixed-departure pilgrimage circuits, small-group international tours, and curriculum-linked school trips.

What hasn't changed is the coordinator model — every group travels with someone from our team, not just a hired driver and a local guide.

Namrata Borse

Senior Tour Manager — Spiritual & Adventure Travel

With over 5–6 years of experience in travel and tour management, Namrata has developed a strong connection with both spiritual journeys and adventure travel across India. Having visited many sacred and holy destinations, she brings a sense of devotion and purpose into her work, designing travel experiences that go beyond sightseeing.

As an experienced trekker — especially across the Sahyadri ranges — she has explored diverse terrains, from challenging trails to offbeat locations, gaining practical knowledge in trekking, safety, and route planning. She also has strong experience managing group tours, ensuring smooth coordination, comfortable stays, and well-organized itineraries — combining trekking expertise with professional tour management to create safe, reliable, and meaningful journeys for every traveler.
